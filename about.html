/ 01 · About the club

We turn students into builders.

The WHRHS Computer Science Club is a student-run engineering collective at Watchung Hills Regional High School. Since 2018 we've been writing code, shipping games and websites, and teaching the next wave of students to do the same.

/ 02 · Mission

Learn it.
Build it.
Ship it.

Tutorials only get you so far. We believe the fastest way to learn computer science is to make real things for real people — then hand what we learned to whoever comes next.

/01

Build real things

Games, tools, and live websites for other WHRHS clubs. Every project ends up in front of actual users.

/02

Teach forward

Through HillsHacks we teach elementary and middle schoolers web dev, pixel art, and game development.

/03

Open to everyone

No experience required. Beginners and competitive programmers work side by side, and everyone ships.
